Features

Everything works without an account. Google sign-in only adds syncing between computers and Google Calendar.

FAP timetable in the popup

Every week of the term, past and upcoming, read from FAP with your own session. Exams stand out, and a status line always says when it was last updated.

Attendance at a glance

Hover a class to see how many sessions you have missed and how many you can still miss before the 20% limit.

Revision checklist

Tick the exams you have finished, right in the FuOverflow and FuExam thread lists.

Pick up where you left off

See which exams you are studying and the question you reached, then jump back to it.

Jump and flag

Jump to any question in the image viewer and flag the ones you are unsure about.

Cover the answer

Put a frosted cover over the answer to test yourself, remembered per exam.

Screenshots with discussion

Capture every question together with its answer discussion, zipped for you.

Sync through Google Drive

Progress, timetable and attendance follow you to another computer, even one not signed in to FAP.

Classes in Google Calendar

Classes and exams go into a separate calendar that follows FAP: changed classes are updated, cancelled ones removed, never duplicated. Reminders by notification, email or both.

Export and import

Move your progress with a JSON file, no account needed.

Where your data is stored

Your data stays in your browser. If you sign in, the extension also keeps a copy in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive and talks to Google directly.

The developer cannot see your data. The extension has no server, analytics or ads.

Google permissions

Google asks for these only if you sign in, and for Calendar only when you turn it on.

openid email profile

Name, email and profile photo

Shown in the popup so you can see which account is syncing.

drive.appdata

Only the extension's own app folder

Lets the extension keep one file, "fu-study-kit.json", in a hidden folder of your Drive. It cannot see or change your other Drive files.

calendar.app.created

Only the calendar it creates

Lets the extension create "FU Study Kit – Lịch học" and manage its events. It cannot read or change your other calendars.

See the privacy policy for details.

Install

Install it from the Chrome Web Store. It takes a few seconds.

  1. Open the storeOpen FU Study Kit on the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Add itClick "Add to Chrome", then "Add extension".
  3. Pin itPin the cat icon from the puzzle menu next to the address bar.
  4. Turn things onOpen a forum page, or click the cat icon to turn on the FAP timetable and Google sync.
